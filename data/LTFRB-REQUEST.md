## Submit through eFOI

1. Open [eFOI](https://www.foi.gov.ph/), register or sign in, and choose
   **Make a Request**.
2. Select **Land Transportation Franchising and Regulatory Board**.
3. Complete the identity-verification requirements using your valid
   government-issued ID. Keep ID details out of the public request text.
4. Copy the title, purpose, and letter below into the relevant fields.
5. Submit the request and save its tracking number and acknowledgment date.

These steps follow the [official FOI guidance](https://www.foi.gov.ph/en/faqs/).
The published standard response period is 15 working days. An agency may notify
you of an extension of up to 20 additional working days. Follow up using the
tracking number if the acknowledged response date passes without a reply.

If LTFRB asks for a signed letter, sign and PDF the same letter below, adding
your contact details in the designated submission fields or letter as required.
A [previous Davao route request](https://www.foi.gov.ph/requests/davao-city-jeepney-routes-and-fare/)
was directed to submit a signed letter and valid ID to Region XI. Follow the
agency's current instructions for the submission channel and attachments.

## Copyable request

**Title:** Current Davao City jeepney and Filcab route master list

**Purpose:** Route-data research for a Davao City commuter-planning prototype.

Dear FOI Receiving Officer,

I am an independent software developer evaluating a commuter route-planning
prototype for Davao City.

I request the latest available master list of authorized jeepney and Filcab
routes within Davao City, including route variants and cross-boundary services
that serve passengers within the city. Please refer this request to LTFRB
Region XI where appropriate.

For each route, please provide the following where available:

1. Official route identifier, name, variants, origin, destination, and approved
   street alignment.
2. Current status—active, suspended, discontinued, or planned—and the date of
   the latest update.
3. Number of authorized units and operating or annually confirmed units,
   identifying which measure is supplied.
4. References to applicable rerouting orders and current fare matrices.

CSV or Excel is preferred. Existing PDFs or scanned records are also welcome.
Please advise whether route geometry or GIS files are available separately.

I located the historical LTFRB-derived inventory dated December 31, 2012,
reproduced in Annex A of the ADB's December 2013 Davao Sustainable Urban Transport
report. I am seeking the current inventory and subsequent revisions.

Please confirm any attribution or permission requirements for using and
displaying the route information in a publicly accessible research prototype.

Personal driver or passenger information is not requested.

Thank you,

[Full name]
